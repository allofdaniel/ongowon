Our values

Three things we hold on to

Art

Painting

We work with artists trained in Korean painting to redraw the motifs and colours of minhwa with a modern eye.

Meaning

Wishes

The carp for success, the peony for prosperity, the crane for long life — every design comes with the story of its wish.

Materials

Fabric

Because it touches a baby's skin every day, we visit mills and makers in person to choose our fabric.

溫故On-go · Learn from the old to understand the new

Ongowon (溫故園) takes its name from the Korean saying ongo-jisin — "learn from the old to know the new." Rather than simply copying old paintings, we wanted to bring back the act of wishing through pictures in a way that fits life today.

At the start of each year, people in Joseon-era Korea hung paintings at home to call in good fortune. Ongowon carries that wish into the first blanket a child is wrapped in.
